Why this game

A small digital tribute to a living craft

Kondapalli Bommalu are hand-carved wooden toys from Kondapalli, Andhra Pradesh. For roughly four centuries, artisan families have shaped lightweight Tella Poniki wood into brightly painted figures inspired by mythology, village life, animals, and everyday Telugu culture.

We chose a carve–paint–display game because the making process is the story. Instead of using Kondapalli dolls only as decoration, the interaction lets you experience how a figure gradually takes form: piece by piece, colour by colour.

The craft is significant because it carries local stories, material knowledge, and skills passed through generations. Kondapalli Bommalu also hold a registered Geographical Indication, linking the toys to their place of origin and artisan community.

This is not a simulation of the artisans’ full expertise. It is a playful introduction designed to spark curiosity about the craft, its process, and the cultural history behind every doll.
